WRITTEN FROM THE HEART

The Birthday Letter

From Ayushi to Harshit • 26 November 2026

26 November 2026 • Midnight Edition

Happy Birthday, my best friend! ❤️

Honestly, I'm not very good at writing these things, but I really wanted to write something for you.

I'm genuinely so happy that I met you in this college. Otherwise, you know how I would've been?😭 College would've definitely been a little different, and honestly, a little more difficult, without all the random conversations, stupid jokes, laughs and little moments we've shared.

It's actually funny how some people slowly become a part of your everyday life without you even realising when it happened. One day, they're just someone you know, just a classmate, and then suddenly, your college days are filled with little moments that you associate with them, especially with me. Random conversations, silly jokes, shared laughter, those little breaks between classes and even the simplest things somehow become memories just because its related with you.

You know, I really appreciate the little things you do. The way you help me, check on me and sometimes make things better without even trying. Because honestly, things just feel a little better automatically when you're around.

And one thing I genuinely love and respect about you is how you're there, not just when everything is happy and fun, but also when I'm sad, nervous, anxious or just having one of those days when nothing feels right. You never had to do something extraordinary. Sometimes, just having you around makes a difference, and I hope you know how much that means to me.

You might not realise it, but you're genuinely special, and you bring so much happiness into the lives of the people around you. I don't think people always realise the impact they have on someone else's life. Sometimes, it's simply about being there, being yourself and making ordinary moments feel a little less ordinary.

I hope this new year of your life brings you everything you've been working towards. I hope you get opportunities that excite you, people who genuinely value you, experiences that make you grow and countless reasons to be proud of yourself.

Never stop being curious, ambitious, funny and slightly annoying and being yourself. 😂 Keep exploring, keep learning, keep making memories and don't forget to enjoy the journey while chasing everything you dream of. And, never underestimate yourself. You're capable of so much more than you sometimes give yourself credit for.

Thank you for being a part of my college memories and for making this little chapter of my life so much more meaningful. I'm genuinely glad our paths crossed. Like, really, thank you God that we met at this time, when we both needed someone its just that we didnt knew that we needed each other in this part of our lives.

And lastly, I hope this little birthday surprise makes you smile. I put a lot of thought, time and effort into it, so you better appreciate it. No returns, no exchanges. 😂

And one more thing, just remember that our friendship genuinely means a lot to me, and I'd probably cry if anything ever happened to it. So please, let's keep making memories and annoying each other for a very, very long time. 🥹

and just wanna tell you again that thank you for coming into my life, actually making me smile and laugh, being so understanding, being my biggest supporter, caring about me.. I really appreciate it. Thank You for just being yourself.

Happy Birthday once again, Harshit!

Stay happy, stay healthy, keep shining and never lose that spark of yours.

With lots of appreciation, Ayushi ❤️
